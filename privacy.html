Privacy Policy

CYRUS · last updated 22 September 2026

Who this covers

CYRUS is a personal, single-user application that runs on its operator's own computer. It has one user: the person who installed it. This policy describes what happens to that person's data when they connect their own Google account.

What is accessed

With the operator's explicit consent, CYRUS requests these Google scopes:

  • gmail.readonly — to read and search their own mail;
  • gmail.send — to send mail as them, at their request;
  • calendar.readonly — to read their own calendar;
  • calendar.events — to create and cancel their own events, when granted.

It accesses only the account that signed in. It does not read anyone else's mailbox or calendar, and it cannot: the token it holds is issued for one account by that account's owner.

Where the data goes

CYRUS runs locally. Mail and calendar data fetched from Google is held on the operator's own machine, in files and local databases under their own user account, and is not uploaded to any server operated by this project. There is no backend, no hosted database, and no analytics.

One exception, stated plainly. CYRUS can use language models to do its work. Depending on how the operator has configured it, a request may be handled either by a model running locally on their own machine, which sends nothing over the network, or by Anthropic's API, in which case the text of that request — which may include the content of a message or an appointment — is transmitted to Anthropic for processing under their terms. Which of the two is used is the operator's own setting.

What is not done

  • Data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.
  • There is no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Data is not used to train any model.
  • Data is not transferred to anyone except as described above.

Limited Use

CYRUS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Anything CYRUS keeps is stored locally and can be deleted by the operator at any time by removing the application's data directory. Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions immediately stops all further access to Google data.
