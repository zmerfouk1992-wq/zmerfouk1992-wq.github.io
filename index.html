CYRUS

A personal assistant that runs on its operator's own computer.

What it is

CYRUS is a single-user application. It is installed and run by one person on their own machine, and it works on that person's own data at their request. It is not a hosted service, there are no accounts to sign up for, and it has no users other than its operator.

What it connects to

With the operator's explicit consent, CYRUS can connect to that same person's Google account in order to:

  • read and search their own Gmail, and send mail as themselves;
  • read their own calendar, and — if granted — create and cancel their own events.

It asks for these permissions through Google's standard consent screen, and they can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
